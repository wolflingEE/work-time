Privacy policy

The app stores work sites, work entries, rates, earnings, comments, advances, expenses and settings on your device. Work data is not sent to the developer. The app requires no registration and contains no ads, analytics or cloud sync.

You create backups and save reports to a location you choose. These files may contain your data in plain text. If you choose cloud storage, that service’s terms apply.

A manual update check retrieves version information from GitHub Pages over HTTPS. Work data and device identifiers are not sent. GitHub serves the APK download. External services may process IP addresses and technical data under their own policies.

If you choose to support the developer, the app opens the external JoolPay website without passing work data. External services process payment data under their own privacy policies. The app does not receive banking details. The payment service may share information about the payer with the recipient.

To remove local data, select Delete all data in Settings. Delete previously exported reports and external backups separately from wherever you saved them.
