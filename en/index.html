From clocking in to reporting

Shift tracking

Enter start and end times or type a duration. Each job site can have its own lunch break setting.

Job sites and rates

Give sites colors and hourly rates, and archive completed sites. Saved shifts keep their original rate.

Earnings and calendar

Find shifts in the calendar or history and see earnings by site and for the date range you select.

Advances and expenses

Track advances, travel tickets, clothing and accommodation separately from your earnings.

Excel in four languages

Choose English, Estonian, Russian or Ukrainian for your report independently of the app language.

Local backups

Save and restore your data using a file. Work records stay on your device. Choose a light or dark theme.

Install

Install on Android

  1. Download the signed APK. You need Android 10 or later.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks for permission to install from this source, give permission to the browser or file manager that opened it.
  3. Confirm the installation, open Work Time and select your language.

Update and keep your data

  1. Create a backup in Work Time settings.
  2. Download the new version and open the APK.
  3. Confirm the update to the existing app. Install over your current version to keep your records and settings.

Android may show a warning because the APK comes from outside Google Play. Download it from the official releases page, verify the SHA‑256 checksum and keep Android’s security protections enabled.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?

Work Time requires no account. Shifts, calculations, Excel exports and backups work offline. Internet access is needed to check for an update manually, download the APK or open an external page.

Why does Android ask about the installation source?

The APK is distributed via GitHub Releases. Android may ask for permission for the browser or file manager that opens it.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

Create a backup in Settings, transfer the file to your new phone and restore it in Work Time.
